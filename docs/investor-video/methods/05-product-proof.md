# Method 05 — Product and Recognition Proof

## Narrative role

Plugg, Pin, and LawyerKit demonstrate Patty's ability to build meaningful products across different Korean workflows. They are not presented as equal strategic priorities to Patty Code/PCCP, and their logos never appear without product context.

## Product cards

Each card is 6–7 seconds and follows this fixed order:

1. 2–3 seconds of authentic product behavior.
2. Product logo.
3. Small `PATTY가 만든 제품` anchor.
4. One post-produced human-benefit sentence.

### Plugg

Evidence source: local `/Users/patrickrho/projects/patty/apps/plugg` and repository documentation.

Approved caption:

> AI 앱과 템플릿을 발견하고 바로 실행하는 플랫폼

Capture app/template discovery and use. Do not assert that every no-code creation workflow described in planning documents is already generally available unless the recorded build demonstrates it.

### Pin

Evidence source: `patty-io/pin` repository and authentic product media. The repository was not present locally during planning.

Approved caption:

> 우리 동네의 ‘지금’을 주민들이 함께 확인하는 실시간 지도

Capture a Korean Naver-map neighborhood view, a time-sensitive local post, and resident verification (`있어요/없어요`) if the authentic build/media supports it. Do not substitute a generic social feed.

Do not deploy or provision Pin infrastructure without explicit approval. Prefer supplied authentic media or a local fixture-backed build.

### LawyerKit

Evidence source: local `/Users/patrickrho/projects/one` / `patty-io/lawyerkit`.

Approved caption:

> 대한민국 법률 업무를 위한 문서 중심 AI 워크스페이스

Capture the document as the center of the experience: drafting or review, a restrained AI assist, and tracked accept/reject if available. Avoid real cases, client names, legal documents, or court identifiers.

## Recognition cards

Use the user's authentic images. Preserve image context long enough for the viewer to understand that these are external recognition, not decorative event photos.

### Elevate

Exact post copy:

```text
ELEVATE FESTIVAL 2025
Top 40+ Hottest Companies · No. 2
Selected by The FoundersPress
```

Do not say Patty won second place in an official Elevate pitch competition.

### COMEUP

Exact post copy:

```text
COMEUP GLOBAL MEDIA AWARDS 2025
BEST OF THE TOP 3
Selected by Arageek · UAE
```

Public reporting states that the awards evaluated 275 participating startups and that each international outlet selected three companies. Do not say Patty was third among 500 startups.

## Transition to flagship

End the montage with this narration:

> 지난 1년, Patty는 서로 다른 현장의 문제를 실제 제품으로 해결해 왔습니다.

Then converge the visual traces into Patty Code/PCCP. The implied sentence is: Patty has built before; now it is concentrating that capability on the flagship opportunity.
