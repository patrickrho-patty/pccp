Master format: 3840×2160, 16:9, 23.976 fps, 5:00. Generated shots are clean plates without readable text. Every caption, interface label, subtitle, logo, and number is added in the finishing timeline.

## Global visual bible

- Genre: Korean near-future techno-thriller evolving into grounded optimism.
- World: recognizable contemporary Seoul, not neon cyberpunk and not a generic American city.
- Palette arc: charcoal/steel and sodium streetlight → controlled cobalt/teal → warm dawn and restrained gold.
- Lens language: opening 35–50 mm, shallow focus and controlled handheld micro-motion; solution 40–65 mm with stable dolly movement; vision 24–35 mm with larger spatial scale.
- Human anchor: fictional Korean developer, early 30s, understated dark workwear, natural face, no celebrity resemblance, no hospital uniform.
- Recurring visual motif: a thin pulse of light that begins as an uncontrolled red path and becomes a bounded cobalt route with visible checkpoints.
- Prohibited: floating gibberish UI, holographic code walls, humanoid robots, text baked into AI imagery, fake government emblems, fake hospital interiors, stock handshake imagery, coins, rockets, or charts flying upward.

## Editorial fallback order

If a generated shot fails, use this order:

1. Regenerate the individual keyframe/clip with the locked composition.
2. Use a shorter clean section of the same clip.
3. Replace it with a still plus controlled 2.5D move.
4. Replace it with authentic product/environment footage.
5. Cut the shot and preserve narration over sound design.

Never keep a visibly broken AI shot merely to prove the film used AI.
