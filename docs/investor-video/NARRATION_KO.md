## Pronunciation and performance notes

- PCCP: first occurrence may be read as “패티 코드 컨트롤 플레인”; later visual text may use “PCCP.”
- DARI: “다리.”
- Plugg: approved Korean brand pronunciation must be confirmed from Patty brand assets before recording; do not guess in the final take.
- Pin: “핀.”
- LawyerKit: “로이어킷.”
- Ergazo: approved Korean brand pronunciation must be confirmed before recording.
- ISMS-P, CSAP, DARI packet terms, model names, and benchmark numbers are not spoken in the main narration.
- Hold approximately 0.8 seconds after “누가 허락했을까요?” and 1.0 second before the final sentence.
- The voice may be a professional Korean narrator or a commercially licensed high-quality synthetic voice. It should not imitate or clone the CEO, 윤강준 원장님, or another identifiable person without explicit permission.
